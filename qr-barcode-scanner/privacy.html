Privacy Policy

QR & Barcode Scanner (package com.tejaayyappa.qrbarcodescanner)
Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what the QR & Barcode Scanner app ("the app") does with information. The short version: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you scan stays on your phone. The app is free and shows ads, and the ad service run by Google collects some information to do that (see section 3).

1. Who we are

The app is published by Danda Teja Ayyappa. Questions about this policy: tejagameshub@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The camera is used only to read QR codes and barcodes. Pictures from the camera are not saved and are not sent anywhere.
  • Pictures you pick. If you scan from a picture in your gallery, or share a picture into the app, it is read on your phone and is not uploaded.
  • Scan history. Your scanned codes, the names you give them, stars, and the dates they were opened are saved on your phone only. You can delete one scan, or clear all of them, in the app. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Bulk lists and settings. Your bulk-scan list, language, theme, and vibrate/beep choices are saved on your phone only.
  • Codes you create. QR codes you make are created on your phone. If you save one, it goes to your gallery. If you export history or a list, the file goes where you choose (for example, through the share sheet).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location, and the app has no sign-in.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and earns money by showing ads from Google AdMob: a banner ad on some screens and an occasional full-screen ad. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

We do not receive your scans, and the ad service does not get the contents of the codes you scan. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form from Google the first time you open it. You can change your answer later in Settings → Privacy settings when that option is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, which can vary by phone), and turn off personalised ads there.

4. Product lookup (Open Food Facts)

For a product barcode, the app offers a Look up product button. Only when you tap it and confirm, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product name. Nothing else from your scan is sent. Like any internet request, the server can see your IP address. Open Food Facts handles it under its own policy.

5. Links and websites you open

If a code contains a web link, the app can check it for warning signs on your phone and, only when you choose to open it, show the page inside the app or in another app. Websites you open are run by other people and have their own privacy practices. We have no control over them. Be careful with links from codes you do not trust.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. The only outside services the app talks to for its own features are the ones named above: Google AdMob, and Open Food Facts when you ask for a product lookup.

8. Children

The app is a general tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Keeping and deleting information

Everything we mention as stored by the app is on your phone. Delete scans in the app, or clear the app's storage, or uninstall it, to remove them. Information held by Google for ads is handled by Google under its policy.
